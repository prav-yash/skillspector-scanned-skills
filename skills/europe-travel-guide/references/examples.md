# Europe Travel Guide Examples

## Example 1: 7-Day First-Time Europe

User asks:
"Plan a 7-day first Europe trip."

Recommended structure:

| Day | City | Plan |
|---:|---|---|
| 1 | Paris | Arrival, Eiffel Tower area, Seine walk |
| 2 | Paris | Louvre or Orsay, Montmartre |
| 3 | Amsterdam | Train to Amsterdam, canal walk |
| 4 | Amsterdam | Museums, Jordaan, food market |
| 5 | Rome | Fly/train depending on route, evening piazza walk |
| 6 | Rome | Colosseum, Roman Forum |
| 7 | Rome | Vatican or local neighborhoods |

Note:
This is fast-paced. A slower version should reduce to 2 cities.

## Example 2: Budget Estimate

User asks:
"Estimate cost for 10 days in Europe."

Use categories:

| Category | Budget Range | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Accommodation | depends on city/style | hostel/hotel/apartment |
| Food | daily estimate | budget/moderate/luxury |
| Transport | route dependent | train/flight/local transit |
| Attractions | depends on museums/tours | check current prices |
| Buffer | 10–15% | recommended |

Use calculator for totals when numbers are provided.
