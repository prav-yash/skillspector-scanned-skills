## Planning Heuristics

- For first-time Europe trips, avoid too many cities.
- For 7 days, usually choose 2 cities or 1 country.
- For 10–14 days, 3–5 cities can work if transport is efficient.
- For rail-heavy trips, keep cities geographically logical.
- Add buffer time before international flights.
- Avoid changing hotels every night unless the user explicitly wants a fast-paced trip.

## Useful Clarifying Questions

Ask only when needed:

- What month or dates are you traveling?
- How many days do you have?
- What is your approximate budget?
- Which city are you flying into and out of?
- Do you prefer trains, flights, or road trips?
- Are you traveling solo, couple, family, or group?
- What interests matter most: history, food, nightlife, nature, shopping, museums, beaches?
- Do you already have a Schengen visa or passport constraints?
